Honest limits

What Zyvora Pharmacy is not, yet.

Taken from the product's own documentation. A long feature list does not prove a commercial launch.

AreaWhere it stands
MaturityA pilot release with a working core, tested at several layers. Start with a controlled pilot alongside your existing process.
ReviewNo pharmacist or CA has reviewed the GST invoices, the H/H1/X register or record retention yet, and no independent security review has been done.
Tax and statutoryGST figures, GSTR-1/3B offline-tool JSON, e-way bill JSON and e-invoice JSON are worked out for you to check. Zyvora files nothing and is not certified GST software; it does not connect to the e-invoice portal (you paste its answer in) or to a GSP. The GSTR-2B match and the exports have not been checked against a real portal statement. Blocked credit, reverse charge and amendments are not built.
PaymentsUPI is a QR for the exact amount: Zyvora cannot see that money arrived, so the cashier confirms in the shop's UPI app. No payment gateway, no SMS sending, no fiscal printer or scale drivers.
HardwareScanners, the camera and thermal printers are tested with simulated input. No real printer or scanner has been tried, and the camera has not been tried on a real phone.
Medicine dataNo bundled clinical database. The shop enters its own interaction rules, and salt and strength lookups use your own catalogue.
Controlled drugsThe register is a faithful record, not a licence. Inspectors' formats and local rules still apply, and the legal position on controlled-drug transfers between stores is yours to confirm.
ImportingProducts, opening stock, customers and supplier balances import from CSV. There are no direct readers for Marg, Busy or Tally exports yet.
Desktop installersSigned, auto-updating installers are not published yet. The desktop app is checked on macOS only; Windows and Linux are not verified. The one-command trial on Windows runs inside WSL 2 and has not been verified on a real Windows PC.
Scale and hostingOne PostgreSQL database and no high availability or point-in-time recovery. Run once on a demo cluster, not yet a production one. It is not a hosted service: one company per deployment.

Questions

Straight answers.

Can I run my pharmacy on it today?

Treat it as a pilot. The counter, stock, purchasing, register and GST workings are built and tested, but filing, payment gateways and hardware drivers are not there. Run it alongside your current process first.

Does it do GST?

It works out bills, returns and credit in the way the law sets them out and exports files for the GST offline tool, the e-way bill portal and Tally. It does not file anything and is not certified GST software.

Does it need the internet?

No. The desktop app and the trial copy run on your own machine. The browser demo runs entirely in your browser.

Where does my data live?

On your computer (a SQLite file) for the desktop app, or in your own PostgreSQL for the server. The trial copy keeps made-up data in a container volume that uninstall deletes.

Is there AI in it?

There is an optional invoice reader that fills a delivery from a photo or PDF, running in the app. Suggestions (reorder, refills) are arithmetic from your own history, and the screen shows the working.

Can I see the source?

The source repository is private. Ask sales what can be shared with evaluators; the trial uses published container images, not the source.

Can customers pay by UPI?

Yes, at the counter: set the shop's UPI ID once, and choosing UPI shows a QR for the exact amount that PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm and other UPI apps open. A bill left on account prints a pay-the-balance QR. Zyvora cannot see the payment arrive, so the cashier checks the shop's UPI app before pressing Checkout.

Can it run several stores?

Yes. Each store keeps its own stock, bills and register, with transfers between stores, a store switcher, and reports for one store or all. Customers and their credit limit are shared across the company.

Is it in Hindi?

The screens and bills can be shown in English or Hindi, or both on one bill. Bill PDFs are in English for now.

Can I bring my existing data?

Products, opening stock with expiry and cost, customers with opening balances, and supplier balances import from CSV with a row-by-row check. There is no direct reader for Marg, Busy or Tally exports yet.
